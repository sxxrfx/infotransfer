InfoTransfer speech assets

Speech configuration: Paradee v1.2, Heart voice, int8

Paradee v1.2: sahilmahendrakar/Paradee-8M-v1.2, revision
ed2934d4df8a4fd891e29584c5e1104649409765, Apache-2.0.
https://huggingface.co/sahilmahendrakar/Paradee-8M-v1.2
https://github.com/sahilmahendrakar/paradee
Paradee is a 7.88M-parameter model distilled from Kokoro-82M. Its af_heart
voice is learned from Kokoro's Heart preset; it is not Kokoro's own output.
The graph contains four voices; InfoTransfer offers only Heart.
The author reports WikiText-103 training sentences (CC BY-SA 3.0).
Teacher model: Kokoro v1.0, hexgrad/Kokoro-82M, Apache-2.0.
https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M
Kokoro's model card credits Koniwa CC BY 3.0 and SIWIS CC BY 4.0 data:
https://github.com/koniwa/koniwa
https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/2353
These source-data terms and credits are distinct from the model-weight license.
Model data is redistributed under its upstream terms. tokens.txt is generated
locally from the model's config.json vocabulary.

English pronunciation data: hexgrad/misaki dataset, Apache-2.0, revision
7a6093fd11124188077f2dc16397cc465466d59a.
InfoTransfer regenerates US/GB lexicons, merging silver then gold entries,
lowercasing words, choosing DEFAULT variants, and adding Kokoro/Misaki names.
This transformation follows the Apache-2.0 sherpa-onnx v2.0.1 generator by
Xiaomi Corp. / Fangjun Kuang. The generated files are modified derivatives.
The generic lexicon-en-us.txt release asset is not used.

Runtime: ONNX Runtime 1.28.2, MIT license, taken from the pinned sherpa-onnx
v2.0.1 arm64 release archive. InfoTransfer calls its C API directly and ships
no sherpa-onnx library. Headers: microsoft/onnxruntime v1.28.2.
See the application third-party notices and exact artifact manifest.

Offline pronunciation utility:

eSpeak NG 1.52.0, revision 4870adfa25b1a32b4361592f1be8a40337c58d6c,
GPL-3.0-or-later, https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng.
The separate phonemize executable statically includes eSpeak NG and its
ucd-tools dependency. It converts new English words to phonemes over local
stdin/stdout pipes; it does not synthesize or play eSpeak audio. InfoTransfer
does not link eSpeak into the Rust application. Original InfoTransfer source,
including native/phonemize.c, remains Apache-2.0; the combined helper is
distributed under GPL-3.0-or-later with its corresponding source.

Build modifications restrict compiled dictionaries to English and honor
disabled Sonic without fetching it. The helper, data, full eSpeak COPYING,
exact patched source, original utility and build instructions are bundled in
Contents/Resources/phonemizer. PROVENANCE.json records artifact hashes and
the upstream revision. Reproduce with scripts/prepare_pronunciation.py.
Only the chosen neural voice generates the audible speech.
